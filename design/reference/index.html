Placeholders

static.wearearchangel.com serves placeholder media for anywhere real content does not exist yet. There is one file per role. Pick the file by what the placeholder is standing in for, not by guessing at a format.

Placeholder files and the role each one is for
File Use for Preview
placeholder.svg Background placeholders. An image filling a card or section as a background layer, with content on top of it.
placeholder.webp Normal images. Inline content photos that do not play a background role.
placeholder.webm Mini or short videos.
placeholder.mp4 Full-length videos.
placeholder.mp3 Audio.
placeholder.pdf Downloadable documents. A real, tiny one-page PDF rather than a stub that returns 404. Open the PDF

Fallback for a failed image

<img
  src="/images/hero.jpg"
  alt=""
  onerror="this.onerror = null; this.src = 'https://static.wearearchangel.com/placeholder.webp'"
/>

Use placeholder.svg as the fallback when the failed image was a background layer, and placeholder.webp for any other image.

Things to know

  • Placeholders are starting points. Replace each one with real content as it becomes available.
  • You may load them from the CDN at runtime, but the files can be renamed or removed. Copy a placeholder into your own project if it must not break.
  • placeholder.mp4 replaced the earlier placeholder.m4v, which no longer exists.
